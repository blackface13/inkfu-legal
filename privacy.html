Inkfu — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-22

Publisher: BlackFace, an independent game developer based in Vietnam ("we", "us")

Contact: bf.blackface@gmail.com (we are reachable by email only)

1. Summary

Inkfu ("the game") needs no account and has no sign-in: your progress is stored on your device. We collect anonymous gameplay statistics and crash reports to improve the game, and Google Play processes your purchases. The game currently shows no ads. We do not sell your data.

2. What we collect

DataExampleWhyWho processes it
Gameplay eventsrun started / ended, floor reached, card picked, upgrade bought, settings changedUnderstand how the game is played, balance it, fix problemsGoogle Firebase Analytics
Device and performance datadevice model, OS version, performance tier, frame-rate samples, crash logs and stack traces, device state at the time of a crashKeep the game stable and fast on your deviceGoogle Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics
App instance identifiera random id generated by Firebase for this installationCount events and deliver game settings without knowing who you areGoogle Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config)
Purchase recordsproduct bought, price tier, order id and purchase tokenDeliver and restore what you boughtGoogle Play Billing (through Unity IAP)

Purchases are paid through Google Play Billing: we never see your card or other payment details, we only receive the order id and the purchase token that prove a purchase.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, files or messages. The game has no account sign-in, no cloud save, no leaderboard and no chat. Gameplay events never contain personal data.

3. Consent and choices

  • Analytics. In the EU/EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before sending any analytics; elsewhere analytics are on by default. You can withdraw or change your choice at any time in Settings › Account & privacy › Privacy.
  • Ads. The game currently shows no ads and contains no advertising SDK; we do not use an advertising identifier and do not personalize ads. If we ever add ads, we will update this policy (and ask for any consent the law requires) before they appear.
  • Notifications. Local notifications only; turn them off in Settings › Notifications or in your device settings.

4. How long we keep data

  • On your device: until you use Settings › Account & privacy › Delete data or uninstall the game.
  • Analytics: 14 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Crash reports: 90 days.
  • Purchase records: as long as Google Play keeps them under its own policy.

5. Deleting your data

Settings › Account & privacy › Delete data (two confirmations) erases your progress, the stored run, analytics logs and performance logs on the device. To delete analytics data already sent, email bf.blackface@gmail.com with the date you installed the game; we will ask Firebase to delete the data linked to your app instance.

6. Children

The game is rated 12+ and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the game, contact us and we will delete the related data.

7. Security and transfers

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our processors may store data outside your country (for example in the United States) under their own safeguards (Google: Standard Contractual Clauses).

8. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config): https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Play: https://policies.google.com/privacy

9. Changes

We will update the effective date above when this policy changes and publish the new text at the same address.

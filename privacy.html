Inkfu — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-24

Publisher: BlackFace, an independent game developer based in Vietnam ("we", "us")

Contact: bf.blackface@gmail.com (we are reachable by email only)

1. Summary

Inkfu ("the game") needs no account and has no sign-in: your progress is stored on your device. We collect anonymous gameplay statistics and crash reports to improve the game, and Google Play processes your purchases. The game shows ads (rewarded videos you choose to watch and ads between runs) through Unity LevelPlay; ads are personalized only where you allow it. We do not sell your data for money.

Purchases are paid through Google Play Billing: we never see your card or other payment details, we only receive the order id and the purchase token that prove a purchase.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, files or messages. The game has no account sign-in, no cloud save, no leaderboard and no chat. Gameplay events never contain personal data.

3. Consent and choices

  • Analytics. In the EU/EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before sending any analytics; elsewhere analytics are on by default. You can withdraw or change your choice at any time in Settings › Account & privacy › Privacy.
  • Ads. In the EU/EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game shows a consent message before personalized ads; without your consent ads stay non-personalized (they may still use data to show, cap and protect ads). In US states with privacy laws you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your data. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Account & privacy › Privacy, reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings, and on iOS turn tracking off in Settings › Privacy › Tracking. Buying Remove Ads or VIP Ad-Free removes ads between runs.
  • Notifications. Local notifications only; turn them off in Settings › Notifications or in your device settings.

4. How long we keep data

  • On your device: until you use Settings › Account & privacy › Delete data or uninstall the game.
  • Analytics: 14 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Crash reports: 90 days.
  • Purchase records: as long as Google Play keeps them under its own policy.
  • Advertising data: kept by Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks under their own policies (linked below).

5. Deleting your data

Settings › Account & privacy › Delete data (two confirmations) erases your progress, the stored run, analytics logs and performance logs on the device. To delete analytics data already sent, email bf.blackface@gmail.com with the date you installed the game; we will ask Firebase to delete the data linked to your app instance.

6. Children

The game is rated 12+ and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children and the ads are not targeted at children. If you believe a child has used the game, contact us and we will delete the related data.

7. Security and transfers

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our processors may store data outside your country (for example in the United States) under their own safeguards (Google, Unity: Standard Contractual Clauses).

8. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config): https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Play: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and Unity Ads: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Google User Messaging Platform (consent message): https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

9. Changes

We will update the effective date above when this policy changes and publish the new text at the same address.
